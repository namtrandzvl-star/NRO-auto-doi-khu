2. Tu danh va chuyen khu khi het quai - nam trong chuc nang Tan Sat, co bat/tat
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ban mod goc da co: tu danh quai gan nhat, va tu chuyen khu khi phat hien boss.
- Them moi, trong lop auto-train (lop chua logic doi khu theo boss):
  + Them bien dem rieng: autoChangeZoneEmptyTicks
  + Them co bat/tat: autoChangeZoneEnabled (mac dinh BAT)
  + Moi lan kiem tra doi khu:
    - Neu dang trong qua trinh doi khu thi khong kich hoat lai.
    - Neu phat hien boss: doi khu nhu cu, hien "Phat hien boss! Chuan bi doi khu..."
      (khong phu thuoc vao co bat/tat o tren).
    - Neu co bat/tat dang TAT: dem het quai reset ve 0, khong doi khu.
    - Neu co bat/tat dang BAT va khong tim thay quai hop le de danh
      (ham tim quai gan nhat tra ve null):
      dem tang len 1. Neu quai xuat hien lai thi dem reset ve 0.
      Neu het quai lien tuc 2 lan kiem tra thi kich hoat doi khu
      giong nhu khi gap boss, hien "Het quai! Chuan bi doi khu..."
    - Sau do may doi khu theo danh sach khu da luu san, xoay vong khu tiep theo.
      Neu khong co khu nao phu hop thi hien "Khong tim thay khu phu hop!" va dung lai.
  + Khong thay doi cach danh, cach chon quai, hay toc do danh.
- Bat/tat trong game: mo menu Auto Train (menu Tan Sat) -> muc moi
    "Doi Khu Khi Het Quai: Bat/Tat"
  Cham vao muc do de doi trang thai; may hien "Doi Khu Khi Het Quai: Bat" hoac
  "...: Tat" va menu cap nhat ngay. Co nay chi co tac dung trong phien choi
  (tat game mo lai se ve mac dinh Bat).
